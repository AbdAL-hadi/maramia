# Maramia UAE · marketing team guide

This guide is for the UAE concept demo. Start at the homepage for the customer journey or open `/marketing` for campaign and feedback tools. Use **English** or **العربية** in the header. The studio and QR tools also let you choose the language of the exported design independently.

## Show the customer journey

- **Menu and offers.** In **Explore Our Menu**, search by dish or ingredient, filter vegetarian entries, and use **View Details** to see ingredients and AED prices. In **Offers**, browse campaign cards and their terms. The values in the demo are provisional.
- **Reservations.** In **Reserve a Table**, select a future date, choose a Dubai time and a party size, and pick a table on the floor plan or from **Available Table List**. Enter sample details and confirm. Open **Manage Reservation** to demonstrate an edit or cancellation in the same browser.
- **Loyalty.** Open **Maramia Rewards**, choose **Join Rewards**, and show the tier and points history. Earn demo points by taking a simulated order to **Completed** in tracking. On a later eligible order, choose a reward at checkout. A promotional code and a reward cannot be combined on one order. The sample memberships are for presenting tiers and examples.
- **Feedback.** Use **Feedback** to submit optional ratings. Open **Customer Feedback Analytics** in the marketing dashboard to see averages and recommendation scores. An unanswered question contributes no rating to its average. The feedback and sample statistics are for the demo.

## Prepare campaign materials

1. **Social Media Content Studio.** Choose a template and **Instagram Post** for a square feed image or **Instagram Story** for a vertical image. Choose the photo, campaign and graphic language. Edit the headline, supporting text and call to action, then check the preview. Select **Download PNG**. Use **Copy Caption**, **Copy Hashtags**, and **Copy Campaign Link** to prepare supporting material. Review the image and its terms before using them outside the demo.
2. **Campaign Link Builder.** Choose the destination, existing campaign, channel, and medium. Give the tracking campaign a short, recognizable name. Use neutral partner labels for legacy referral links. Select **Generate Campaign Link**, then **Copy Link**, **Open Link**, or **Save Link Settings**. Saved settings belong to this browser.
3. **QR Code Generator.** Select the destination or use **Generate QR Code** in the link builder to pass across the campaign link. Adjust the title and graphic language. Generate the card, then choose **Download PNG** or **Print QR Card**. Scan the exported card before printing. A QR code pointing to localhost needs an approved reachable website address for phone visitors.
4. **Analytics and CSV.** Open `/marketing` and check **Dataset** first. **Local interactions** shows demo activity from this browser. **Synthetic sample** shows the separate illustrative dataset. Filter by date, campaign and acquisition source. Use **Export Aggregate CSV**. The CSV identifies which dataset generated it. Gift previews, exports, and link generation are operational activity. Sales totals reflect the simulated order flow. **Reset Analytics** clears local analytics and feedback without clearing the cart, orders, reservations, loyalty balance, or saved link configurations.

## Approvals before external use

| Item | Marketing approval needed |
| --- | --- |
| Official logo and brand assets | Supply the approved logo files, colors, typography guidance, and social sharing assets. The current mark is a temporary original concept. |
| Restaurant imagery | Approve imagery and usage rights. Concept images illustrate a proposed location and dishes. |
| UAE menu prices and delivery coverage | Approve AED prices, location-specific availability, delivery areas, costs, and pickup policy. |
| Campaign content and promotional terms | Approve campaign copy, creative, codes, dates, eligibility, minimums, and channel use. |
| UAE location, contact information, and opening hours | Supply an approved address, operating hours, phone, email, and social accounts. UAE fields currently show pending confirmation. |
| Loyalty rules | Approve earning rates, tiers, reward terms, redemption criteria, and program policy. |

Keep real guest details and payment information out of the demo. The browser uses local data, and contact form entries are validated on the page and then cleared. The marketing workspace is open for presentation use.
